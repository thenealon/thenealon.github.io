Every displayed equivalent is checked against the same truth function. There are infinitely many syntactic equivalents, so the search is capped at a useful finite number.

Also accepts not, and, or, xor, nand, nor, implies, iff, if P then Q, and symbols such as !, &&, ||, ->, and <->. Use up to six variables.
